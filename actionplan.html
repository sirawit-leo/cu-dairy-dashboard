score = impact × urgency weight ÷ cost weight. Cost divides, urgency multiplies — so a medium-cost action needs ~2.6× the impact of a free one to rank alongside it. Impact is a 1–10 ranking score, not a forecast: how much of the herd's measured shortfall does this sit in front of? A pool addressed is not a saving projected.

The service and conception behind each calving are reconstructed, not recorded — the source holds three independent event lists, so a service is matched to a calving by gestation window and a conception by confirmation window. The match is checked rather than assumed: it yields a gestation median of 275 days and a service-to-confirmation median of 39 days. Seven of 56 calvings have no matching service and seventeen no confirmed conception; those are left blank rather than filled in. Monthly conception counts are right-censored at the end of the window, because a service in July is confirmed in August. Figures are provided for academic discussion.
