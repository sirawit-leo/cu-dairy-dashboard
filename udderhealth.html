The author cannot grant permission for any use of the data. Read the figures with their limits in view. The herd's automatic recording system failed between 27 April and 16 July 2026; no per-cow milk yield or body weight was recorded in that window, and weight recording never resumed. Values spanning that gap are estimated and marked ≈ — typical error around 2.6 kg/day for yield. Fifteen samples with uncountable SCC or physiologically impossible values are excluded from every figure. No treatment outcome is recorded anywhere in this dataset, so nothing here evaluates whether a treatment worked; every “cured” figure is an SCC crossing between two tests roughly a fortnight apart. The record covers eight months of a single production year, so no seasonal effect can be separated from a one-off management change. Groups shown by parity or by pen can be small — read counts rather than rates wherever a group falls below about twenty animals. Figures are provided for academic discussion. They are not a substitute for veterinary judgement and should not be used alone to make decisions about an individual animal.
