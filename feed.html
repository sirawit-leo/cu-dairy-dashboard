How big is the shortfall really? Two answers, and they disagree

This is left contested on purpose rather than resolved.

What the file says

14 of 48 cows short of energy, 10 short of protein, on the workbook's own requirement columns (KCF 2011).

This is what the page charts above.

What the body-condition check says

The deficits the file implies would mean losing 2.2–6.1 BCS units over the period. Body condition actually rose about 0.25. A deficit that large is not physiologically possible, so the requirement column runs high, the recorded intake runs low, or both.

From the feeding-management project's own reconciliation. It is a ΔBCS-to-energy check, not a recomputed NRC requirement — this project does not recompute NRC.

What survives both readings: protein is genuinely short, the highest-yielding cows are the ones short of it, and a flat ration across a pen guarantees it. What does not survive is the size of the energy gap.
