The energy behind the milk — FCM 4%, ECM and feed efficiency (พลังงานที่ใช้สร้างน้ำนม)

Two tanks of the same weight need not have cost the same energy to fill. Milk is standardised first and divided by intake second — and each result is read against the benchmark set for its own basis.

their §5 (id s6)

Which basis, which benchmark, and where each one comes from

A benchmark has to sit on the same basis as the number it is judging. An efficiency computed on ECM read against a raw-milk range is wrong the moment it is written down, so every basis here carries its own range and its own citation.

What the feeding work found, ranked by weight of evidence

Three findings rest on several independent instruments agreeing. Three rest on one measurement each and are marked as such.

  • Protein in the mix fell and never came back solid
    Four independent lines say the same thing. The TMR fell 17.2% → 15.0% crude protein at the 11 May round and the concentrate 22.6% → 18.4% with it (FeedQuality). MUN fell to 6.70 in the tank the same month (BulkTank). 14 of 48 cows sit under the farm's own 10–14 band in August (FeedCowAug). And the ration now sits under the 16% floor this project set for it.
  • One flat ration cannot cover the pen it is fed to solid
    34 of the 48 milking cows need a denser ration than the mix delivers, and the mix is already near the practical ceiling. Energy offered is constant inside a pen while requirement rises with yield, so 14 cows come out short of energy in a month when every pen average clears its requirement. The problem is the flatness, not the total.
  • Efficiency is falling, and the herd is eating the same to get less solid
    Feed efficiency on ECM fell from 1.537 to 1.085, 29% over eight months, with no month recovering. Intake held between 14.15 and 17.34 kg while milk fell from 23.80 to 16.00 kg.
  • The pen that needs the most feed is the one running closest to empty provisional
    High โคนมมาก leaves 1.83% in the trough against Cornell's 2% floor, while the other two milking pens leave over 7%. But see the caveat under the table: this is one reading repeated across three month blocks, not a trend.
  • The mix is finer than it should be provisional
    The TMR holds 23.70% on the 4 mm sieve against a 10–20% standard and 29.05% in the pan, and peNDF8 has fallen to 23.12 — the bottom of the 21–24 working range. Milk fat has not moved with it, so this is a risk on the record rather than a problem already showing.
  • Part of the milk loss is not a feeding problem at all provisional
    Mean days in milk went from 132 to 193 over the eight months. At this herd's own decline rate that accounts for about 49% of the milk lost, and it is a reproduction result arriving in the feed figures. Fixing the ration will not recover that share.

Split so that nothing here is proposed on evidence that cannot carry it.
