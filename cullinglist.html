The service and conception behind each calving are reconstructed, not recorded — the source holds three independent event lists, so a service is matched to a calving by gestation window and a conception by confirmation window. The match is checked rather than assumed: it yields a gestation median of 275 days and a service-to-confirmation median of 39 days. Seven of 56 calvings have no matching service and seventeen no confirmed conception; those are left blank rather than filled in. Monthly conception counts are right-censored at the end of the window, because a service in July is confirmed in August. Figures are provided for academic discussion. They are not a substitute for veterinary judgement and should not be used alone to make decisions about an individual animal.
